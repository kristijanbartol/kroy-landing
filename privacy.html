Privacy

What this site collects, which is almost nothing.

The site itself

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookies. Nothing is stored in your browser. There is no form on the page: the only way to send us anything is to write to us.

Two third parties your browser contacts

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps ordinary server logs, including the IP address a request came from. Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google and Google therefore receives that request. Neither is under our control and we read neither.

If you send us a size chart

We use what you send only to produce the assessment you asked for. We do not publish it, pass it on or use it to train anything. We ask for no customer data and we do not want any. Write to us and we will delete what you sent, at any time and without asking why.
