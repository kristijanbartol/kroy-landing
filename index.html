A “Medium” isn’t a Medium.

The same label means different things across brands, materials and cuts. Customers can’t tell, so they guess, and a large share of fashion orders come back, almost always over size and fit.

The fix isn’t another size chart. It’s one consistent, comparable measure of fit that works for every product you sell, including the ones with thin or messy data.

The idea, in one move

Four brands. The same “M”. Four different fits.

Online, four pairs of jeans all just say “Size M”, so they look interchangeable. KROY reads the brand, size and material - no photo needed - and places each one on a single real waist scale, revealing how it actually fits.
