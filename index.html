Are these your customers?

No. These are bodies we built to fit this chart's size M, and every count here counts those women and nothing more. Send your own customer figures and we will weight it to them.

What we do not claim

Nobody has worn these, so we will not call this validated. It is a measurement of the garment against the body, checked by a cloth simulation that knew nothing about how the garment was designed and lands on the same answer. The draping makes the picture. It never decides a verdict.
